Our Story

Welcome to Island Spice Cafe, where the vibrant flavors of Guyana come to life. Our journey began with a simple dream - to share the rich culinary traditions of the Caribbean with the world.

Guyana, the only English-speaking country in South America, is a melting pot of cultures - East Indian, African, Indigenous, Chinese, and Portuguese. This beautiful diversity is reflected in every dish we serve.

From our signature Pepperpot - Guyana's national dish - to our freshly made Pholourie and Bake and Saltfish, every recipe is crafted with authentic ingredients and generations of love.

Our Guyanese Heritage

Guyana's cuisine is a reflection of its rich cultural diversity. Here are some of the influences that inspire our menu:

Indo-Guyanese

Curries, roti, and sweets like Gulab Jamun

Afro-Guyanese

Pepperpot, metemgee, and cassava-based dishes

Indigenous

Cassareep, cassava bread, and jungle spices

Chinese & Portuguese

Fried rice, chow mein, and garlic-infused dishes
